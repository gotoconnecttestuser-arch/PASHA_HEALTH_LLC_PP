PASHA HEALTH LLC – Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 01/02/2026

1. Information We Collect

PASHA HEALTH LLC may collect the following information:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Appointment or scheduling information
- Information submitted through forms, phone calls, text messages, or in-person interactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for business and customer care purposes, including:

- Responding to inquiries
- Conversational SMS communications
- Appointment scheduling and confirmations
- Customer support and follow-up communications
- Service-related notifications and updates

Your information will not be used for marketing or promotional SMS messages without your explicit consent.

3. SMS Communications & Consent

By providing your phone number and verbally opting in, you consent to receive conversational and customer care SMS messages from PASHA HEALTH LLC.

Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
Reply HELP for assistance.

4. Sharing of Information

PASHA HEALTH LLC does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only with trusted service providers that assist us in operating our communication and SMS services, and only as necessary to provide those services. These providers are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information.

Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Protection of Information

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel only.

6. Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message.

To request updates or deletion of your information, please contact PASHA HEALTH LLC directly.

7. Updates to This Policy

PASHA HEALTH LLC may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
